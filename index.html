Questions, answered

How long do press-on nails last?

With nail glue, 1 to 2 weeks. With adhesive tabs, 2 to 3 days. Gently removed sets can be reused.

How do I find my nail size?

Measure the widest part of each nail in millimetres and send the sizes on WhatsApp after ordering. We send a size guide too.

How long does a custom set take?

5 to 7 working days to make, plus delivery across India.

Can you copy a design from a photo?

Yes. Choose "I have a design", upload it, describe the changes and send us the image on WhatsApp.
