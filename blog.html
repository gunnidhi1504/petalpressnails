How to Apply and Remove Press-On Nails Safely

A beginner-friendly guide to beautiful nails and better nail care.

Press-on nails are a convenient way to enjoy beautiful, stylish nails without having to visit a salon every time. Whether you love minimal designs, elegant French tips, or creative nail art, press-on nails let you change your look with ease.

At Petal Press, we believe that great nails should also come with proper care. If you are new to press-on nails, this guide will help you understand how to prepare your natural nails, apply your press-ons, maintain them and remove them carefully.

1. What Are Press-On Nails?

Press-on nails are artificial nail extensions designed to fit over your natural nails. They are available in different shapes, lengths, colours and designs.

They are popular because they offer flexibility and allow people to experiment with different nail styles. Custom press-on nails can also be designed according to your preferred shape, colour, length and overall aesthetic.

2. Prepare Your Natural Nails Before Application

Preparing your nails properly can help create a cleaner application. Before applying press-on nails, follow these simple steps:

  1. Wash your hands with soap and water and dry them completely.
  2. Remove any old nail polish from your natural nails.
  3. Gently shape your natural nails using a nail file.
  4. Push back cuticles gently only if needed. Avoid cutting them.
  5. Choose the press-on nail size that fits each natural nail comfortably.
  6. Make sure your natural nails are clean and dry before application.
Quick tip: Avoid applying press-on nails over damaged, painful or infected nails. If you notice irritation or an unusual nail problem, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

3. How to Apply Press-On Nails

Once your natural nails are prepared, you can begin the application process. Always follow the instructions supplied with your particular press-on nail kit and adhesive.

Step 1: Select the Correct Sizes

Match each artificial nail with the corresponding natural nail. The press-on should fit comfortably without pressing against the surrounding skin.

Step 2: Choose Your Adhesive

Depending on your kit, you may use nail adhesive tabs or nail glue. Read the product instructions carefully. Use only the amount recommended by the manufacturer and avoid getting adhesive on your skin.

Step 3: Position the Nail

Align the press-on nail near the cuticle area without placing it directly on the skin. Gently position it over your natural nail.

Step 4: Press and Hold

Apply gentle, even pressure according to the adhesive instructions. Avoid moving the nail while the adhesive is setting.

Step 5: Repeat Carefully

Repeat the process for the remaining nails. Check that each nail feels comfortable and is properly positioned.

4. Tips to Make Your Press-On Nails Last Longer

Good maintenance can help keep your press-on nails looking neat. Here are some useful habits:

  • Follow the adhesive manufacturer's application instructions.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to water immediately after application, as directed by your adhesive instructions.
  • Wear protective gloves when using household cleaning products.
  • Do not use your nails as tools to open cans or packages.
  • Avoid picking, pulling or biting your press-on nails.
  • Check your nails regularly for lifting, discomfort or irritation.

5. How to Remove Press-On Nails Safely

Removing press-on nails carefully is just as important as applying them correctly. Never pull or forcefully rip off artificial nails, as this may damage your natural nail surface.

  1. Check the removal instructions provided with your nail adhesive or kit.
  2. Use the recommended removal method for the adhesive you selected.
  3. If soaking is recommended, use the product and method specified in its instructions.
  4. Allow the adhesive to loosen before gently attempting removal.
  5. Never force a nail that remains firmly attached.
  6. After removal, gently clean your natural nails and moisturise your hands and nails.
Important: Different adhesives require different removal methods. Do not assume that every press-on nail can be removed using the same soaking process. Stop if you experience pain, burning or irritation.

6. How to Take Care of Your Natural Nails

Your natural nails deserve attention even when you are not wearing press-ons. A few simple habits can support good nail care:

  • Keep your nails clean and dry.
  • Moisturise your hands and nails regularly.
  • Avoid biting your nails or picking at the surrounding skin.
  • Do not cut or aggressively push back your cuticles.
  • Give your natural nails breaks between artificial nail applications.
  • Pay attention to redness, swelling, pain or other unusual reactions.

7. Common Press-On Nail Mistakes to Avoid

  • Choosing the wrong size: A nail that is too wide or too narrow may feel uncomfortable.
  • Applying on wet nails: Make sure your natural nails are completely dry before application.
  • Using too much adhesive: Follow the instructions provided with your nail product.
  • Forcing removal: Pulling off firmly attached nails can cause damage.
  • Ignoring irritation: Stop using the product if you notice an unusual reaction.

8. Final Thoughts

Press-on nails make it easier to explore different nail styles and express your personality. With the right preparation, careful application and gentle removal, you can enjoy your nail designs while paying attention to your natural nail health.
