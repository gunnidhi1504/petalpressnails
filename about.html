Welcome to Petal Press

We believe that nail styling should be creative, convenient and personal.

Whether you enjoy simple everyday looks or love experimenting with colours, shapes and nail art, press-on nails make it easy to switch up your style. Petal Press brings together ready-made designs and a design studio where you can create a look inspired by your own ideas.

What we offer

Custom press-on nails

Use the design studio to explore shapes, lengths, colours and finishes and build a nail look that reflects your taste.

Ready-made designs

Browse a collection of press-on nail styles when you want inspiration or a design that is ready to choose.

Handmade and reusable

Our press-on nail sets are handmade and designed to be reusable, so you can enjoy your chosen look again with proper care.

Our approach

Personal style looks different for everyone. That is why we celebrate choices: from nail shape and length to colour and finish. Whether you arrive with a clear idea or are still exploring, our goal is to make discovering your next nail look enjoyable.

Ordering from Petal Press

When you find a design you love, you can use the website to explore your options and place an order through WhatsApp. Please check the product and ordering details on the website before confirming your order.
